## Source-confirmed gap and strictly negative-only correction

The real `MailboxFourBoundResultLeavesArchiveV1.captureInside` reads each of four result leaves as **1–512 raw bytes** and serializes `BYTES_BASE64=` within a bounded four-kind portable archive. Independently, the G21.129 `MailboxSevenPlaneContinuousPhysicalWitnessV1.physicalDigest` captures SHA-256 and length for the exact same names, with NOFOLLOW metadata before/after each read. G21.155 checks that portable and physical *names* match but does not check that their *raw bytes* agree. Thus two fully self-hashed, filename-aligned retained snapshots can coherently disagree about the bytes and still appear to MATCH.

G21.156 extends G21.155's in-memory `requireV3PortableLeafBinding` with **strict, canonical Base64** decoding. Reject empty, malformed, noncanonically padded/unpadded, encoded length >684, decoded length <1 or >512 as `BAD_V3_PORTABLE_BASE64`. For all four recognized v3 result kinds, compare decoded byte length against the retained `PhysicalIdentity.size` and locally recomputed SHA-256 against `PhysicalIdentity.sha256`; failures become checked `V3_PORTABLE_PHYSICAL_SIZE_MISMATCH` or `V3_PORTABLE_PHYSICAL_SHA_MISMATCH`. Retain all G21.144–155 source-family/name/UUID/portable self-SHA validation and original internal BEFORE/AFTER comparison. No new disk IO, OS lease, writer, archive, account state, grant or recovery authority introduced.

**Crucial scope:** The archive and physical identity are both caller-retained in memory, not independently authenticated. A caller controlling both can substitute a new valid byte stream plus matching SHA, size and metadata. Positive matching means observational consistency only and does NOT prove original source provenance, crash durability, writer exclusion or settlement safety.
