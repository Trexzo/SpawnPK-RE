**Safety finding:** The G21.73/JVM/OS per-account publication lock covers final strict account *publication*, but `StrictDurablePlayerSnapshotWriter` creates, writes, forces and may pause on the temporary file **before acquiring that lock**. A cross-process or in-process writer stalled in `BEFORE_ATOMIC_REPLACE` could have a temp that is *months old*, yet still legitimately resume and atomically replace the canonical account. Filename, age, and taking the publication lock therefore **do not establish exclusive ownership of an orphan-looking temp**. Deleting such a temp could make an active writer fail or corrupt a planned publication.

**Implemented** `MailboxOrphanPublicationCleanupAudit` is opt-in, account-canonical, bounded to 48 publication-temp candidates, and scans only the exact account-qualified G21.66 strict temp, G21.83 intent temp and G21.86 COMMIT temp patterns. It runs under the existing bounded G21.73 NOFOLLOW ancestry-verified cooperating advisory lock, uses `DirectoryStream` plus `BasicFileAttributes` with NOFOLLOW, records physical file keys, compares canonical account/journal/COMMIT identities before and after, and checks candidate identity before and after each record. It classifies same-file hard-link aliases as `CANONICAL_HARDLINK_ALIAS_KEEP`, apparently unlinked temps as `POSSIBLY_ACTIVE_OR_ORPHAN_KEEP`, unsafe symlink/directories as `NONREGULAR_OR_SYMLINK_KEEP`, and missing physical keys as `MISSING_PHYSICAL_IDENTITY_KEEP`. Every result and every candidate sets all deletion, mutation, reward, replay, session and ACK authorization to **false**. **It exposes no unlink, cleanup, repair, move or promote operation.** It does not read arbitrary temp contents and does not sweep foreign accounts. Unsafe/nonregular canonical evidence or scan over-capacity aborts fail-closed.
