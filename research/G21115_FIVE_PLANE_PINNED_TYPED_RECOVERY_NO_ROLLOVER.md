This artifact is not written to disk and does not authenticate its own external expected hash. The five checked content planes are: original portable v1 archive with pinned SHA-256; typed v2 capture with separately pinned SHA-256 and `OBSERVED_TYPED_PAIRED_NO_AUTHORITY` / no mixed untyped v1 requirement; canonical exact account bytes pinned by SHA-256; PREPARED journal bytes or explicit ABSENT pin; disk COMMIT bytes or explicit ABSENT pin. Under one G21.100 lifecycle-locked observation with existing inner G21.39 publication locks, v2 is captured BEFORE and AFTER live v1 capture; canonical/sidecars are checked before and after; orphan/canonical physical witness is checked at both ends. All of this repeats in a second lifecycle-locked pass with no locks held during a caller-supplied between-pass callback. Any missing, malformed, foreign, drifted, unexpectedly added or changed evidence plane fails closed. Legacy G21.106/107/108 APIs retain exactly their previous signatures and no typed-v2 requirements.
