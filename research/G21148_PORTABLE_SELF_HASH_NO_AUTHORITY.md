# G21.148 — bounded v1/v2/v3 portable SHA self-consistency (NO AUTHORITY)

**Certified parent:** G21.147 [Draft PR #2474](https://github.com/Trexzo/SpawnPK-RE/pull/2474), exact `00545c98c0112f528b0742a3ef2507b98d7ad4c4`, [Actions #38296729489](https://github.com/Trexzo/SpawnPK-RE/actions/runs/38296729489) SUCCESS Java11 focused 447/447. Issue #2475.

**Distinct defect:** G21.146 checked non-null bounded report structure, G21.147 checked consistency BETWEEN BEFORE and AFTER in each physical Report, but the historical comparator never independently recomputed a portable archive Snapshot's SHA-256 from the actual in-memory portable data. Corruption of a retained v1/v2/v3 body with its old SHA can evade G21.147 if that body is changed on BOTH BEFORE and AFTER snapshots within the same report, leaving that report's in-memory comparisons stable. A changed digest can similarly remain consistent between both snapshots. This is an internal integrity gap, not proof of disk tampering.

**Read-only production fix:** In `MailboxSevenPlaneHistoricalIdentityDeltaV1.requireReport`, after G21.146 structural validation and G21.147 internal BEFORE/AFTER drift classification, recheck *each original* v1, v2 and v3 `Snapshot.portable` against its own `Snapshot.sha256`. The byte encoding is the same canonical US-ASCII used in the underlying G21.105/G21.115/G21.125 writers. Require nonempty 7-bit ASCII data, bounded lengths (v1/v2 32768 bytes; v3 4000), and exactly 64 lowercase hexadecimal digest characters matching the recomputed SHA-256. Any mismatch throws checked `IOException` with `HISTORICAL_IDENTITY_DELTA_UNSAFE_NO_AUTHORITY PORTABLE_SHA_MISMATCH`; invalid length, empty body or non-ASCII input produces `BAD_PORTABLE_LENGTH_OR_ENCODING`. All operations are bounded in memory, performed on supplied old/current report objects. No disk access, G21.100 lease acquisition, archive modification, writer changes, or authorization flags.

**Integration 447→448:** `G21148MailboxPortableSelfHashIntegrationTest` constructs genuine four-result-bound World evidence and all independent pinned original v1/v2/v3/account/journal/COMMIT, then includes inherited G21.144 six real historical inode changes, G21.145 24 simulated absent fileKeys, G21.146 75 malformed report fields and G21.147 36 internal before/after contradictions. New targeted cases total **45**: (1) each of three archive versions × four historical/current BEFORE/AFTER positions × two damaged fields (portable body or claimed SHA) = 24, retaining G21.147's INTERNAL_BEFORE_AFTER_DRIFT veto; (2) each archive × both historical/current Report × both fields changed identically on its own BEFORE and AFTER = 12, requiring the NEW PORTABLE_SHA_MISMATCH veto despite no G21.147 internal drift; (3) each archive × empty/non-ASCII/oversized portable body changed identically in both historical captures = 9, requiring the NEW BAD_PORTABLE_LENGTH_OR_ENCODING veto. Corruption uses test-only in-memory reflection and exact restoration in finally; every restored report then must MATCH with zero changed/uncertain labels and no cleanup/grant authority. Real original World account and archives never changed during those 45 cases; no active JVM lease/watch or live reward grants.

**Critical limitations:** An adversary who forges a portable body and ALSO recomputes a matching SHA can still construct a self-consistent, untrusted in-memory report. SHA self-consistency is **not** independently authenticated source authority, historically complete RAW/default/future writer evidence, identity attestation, atomic cross-file epoch, power-loss durability, archive pruning/rollover/cleanup/unlink, grant/replay/session/release/client ACK. It must never be presented as a substitute for signed independent pins or a historical write census. Frozen R25 #1847 and G21.87/88 untouched.
