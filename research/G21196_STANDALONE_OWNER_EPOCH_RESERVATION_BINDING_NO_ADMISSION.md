## New *UNUSED* two-stage design primitive

`LocalDurableOwnerEpochPrototype` introduces separate fixed sibling records `.g21196-epoch-reservation` and `.g21196-epoch-binding`, on disposable accounts only. It is not G21.188 ACTIVE review and is **never consulted by admission or World persistence**. Pre-registration `reserveBeforeWorldRegistration` exclusively creates a bounded canonical record with exact normalized account path hash, a random correlation UUID and `RESERVED_NO_ADMISSION`; forces the file and parent directory, validates same object/readback. Post-registration `bindAfterWorldRegistration` uses the already observed reservation and an explicit positive generation to exclusively create a second canonical `BOUND_NO_ADMISSION` record containing the original reservation digest, account fingerprint and generation, with independent file+directory force and exact readback. A missing binding, duplicate reservation, duplicate binding, malformed leaf, digest mismatch or uncertain write cannot provide any positive authority; no release or retirement APIs exist.

**Important restrictions:** The `prototypeEpoch` UUID and serialized generation are *not* authenticated ownership proofs, protected locks, a transactional compare-and-set, or a durable credential. A process reading those files can potentially forge their contents. The helper is not wired to `LocalSession`, the existing ACTIVE marker, World, account repository, final-save receipt or any runtime admission decision. Its existence must never permit marker cleanup, account admission or reward/settlement/replay. Even a complete pair does not prove its World generation was assigned to the correct account owner; the test uses synthetic generation 7, not actual runtime registration. No hostile replacement/unlink, power-loss durability or raw writer exclusion is asserted.
