# G21.104 — bounded v1 evidence retention with preflight capacity and mixed-version veto

Certified parent G21.103 `2d05483c73f64822c1ed74ad1dc016ee00ccbfc0`, [Actions #38127620357](https://github.com/Trexzo/SpawnPK-RE/actions/runs/38127620357) SUCCESS, 403 focused Java11. Issue #2387.

**Issue:** G21.103 has a safe-to-read **32 record ceiling** but `runRecorded` previously appended BEGIN and performed caller work before checking that the matching COMPLETE could fit. That could leave a successful opt-in operation with an unrepresentable completion after 16 attempts. Additionally a new attempt could follow an unmatched crash BEGIN without explicit preflight. Neither missing data nor mixed versions may become accidental proof of writer quiescence or entitlement to cleanup.

**Change:** Under the **same existing G21.100 per-account JVM+OS lifecycle lock** and *before* publishing new BEGIN, the opt-in `MailboxWriterAttemptEvidenceV1.runRecorded` now calls its exact, bounded, NOFOLLOW v1 inspector. It refuses an existing unmatched BEGIN (`G21.104 PRIOR_INCOMPLETE_WRITER_ATTEMPT_NO_ENROLL`) and refuses if fewer than two leaf slots remain (`G21.104 WRITER_EVIDENCE_RETENTION_FULL_NO_ENROLL`). Existing unknown versions, corrupted records, account-binding mismatches, orphan COMPLETE and over-capacity inputs already throw via the G21.103 inspector, so **no callback may run**. An accepted opt-in action has room for both its immutable BEGIN and COMPLETE markers. The report now exposes immutable integer diagnostics `recordCount`, `remainingRecordSlots`, and `additionalCompleteAttemptSlots`—none authorize cleanup or imply deployment-wide participation.

**Retention boundary:** This is strictly **append-only, finite evidence retention**, not automatic pruning or rolling window. Up to **16 complete BEGIN+COMPLETE pairs** fit the 32-leaf bound per account. Once full, new opt-in operations fail closed; there is NO silent truncation, deletion, compaction, reseeding, rotation or attempt-to-reward promotion. Failed or killed attempts leave durable BEGIN and block further enrollment until a separately proven reconciliation protocol exists. A v2 or foreign-account record within the versioned namespace blocks writes before callbacks, including when the account also has valid v1 evidence. A legacy account may explicitly opt into its FIRST v1 attempt, but completed records cannot prove that older versions participated.

**Hosted regression:** 15 successful runs yield exactly 30 immutable records and two remaining slots. The 16th run consumes the reserved last pair; a 17th callback is never invoked and no evidence changes. A child JVM hard-exits with `Runtime.halt(126)` after its BEGIN; an **independent JVM** refuses a new attempt before callback and preserves the unmatched record. Mixed v1+v2, malformed v1, and account-crossed v1 records all block operations before action. An account without v1 may opt in once, without retroactive historical proof. All records remain byte-stable and canonical account files are unchanged. Java 11 focused 403→404, standalone Gradle task `g21104MailboxBoundedEvidenceRetentionRegression`.

**Limits:** Bounded v1 ledger exhaustion can deny future opt-in operations; this is intentional until durable archival/rotation and global writer-population fencing are proved. The ordinary World account strict save, PREPARED journal and COMMIT hot paths remain **not** enrolled, to avoid unreviewed extra forced writes. Records describe only opt-in activity, not hardware power-loss guarantees, cleanup permission, session restoration, reward grant, replay or ACK. G21.87–88 and frozen R25 #1847 remain. Keep PR Draft/unmerged until exact-head hosted CI.
