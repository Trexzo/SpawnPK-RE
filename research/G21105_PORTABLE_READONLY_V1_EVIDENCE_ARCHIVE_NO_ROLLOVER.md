# G21.105 — portable read-only opt-in v1 evidence archive (NO ROLLOVER)

Certified parent G21.104 `b1092f4c737be961f0014a7dd41b0c8f05f4336b`, [Actions #38132915772](https://github.com/Trexzo/SpawnPK-RE/actions/runs/38132915772) SUCCESS 404 focused Java11. Issue #2389.

**Purpose:** Before considering retention rollover, capture the exact source record bytes as a reproducible and independently verifiable **in-memory** snapshot. This is a deliberately **read-only** forensic/export boundary; it neither archives records onto disk automatically nor authorizes deletion, compaction or rollover of the 32-leaf v1 evidence ledger.

**Implementation:** `MailboxWriterEvidencePortableArchiveV1.capture` resolves the canonical account once, acquires G21.100's bounded, NOFOLLOW JVM/OS cooperating lifecycle lock, invokes the exact G21.103/104 bounded account/version/payload scanner **before and after** capture, and refuses absent v1 records, malformed/foreign/unknown-version/mixed evidence or exceeding 32 leaves. It reads each exact record under NOFOLLOW `FileChannel` with bounded 512-byte payload and physical file-key/size/mtime checks, sorts records by exact filename and serializes a strict 32 KiB ASCII manifest with explicit format version, account, original negative-only ledger status, begin/completion counts, exact record names and canonical Base64 of full record bytes, plus an explicit no-authority trailer. It returns a SHA-256 digest over the entire canonical manifest.

`verifyPortable(manifest,trustedSha256)` independently parses the self-contained text without file I/O, verifies its SHA-256, bounded canonical ASCII/base64, v1-only strict filenames and payloads, bound account and UUID, sorted unique record ordering, BEGIN-before-COMPLETE evidence semantics, exact counts and status, and explicit no-cleanup policy. A mismatching digest or self-rehashed but inconsistent manifest fails closed. **The expected hash must be pinned separately through a trusted channel; an unsigned hash delivered alongside untrusted content does NOT establish provenance.** A digest match verifies only the exported bytes at the time of capture, not that historical or future uncooperative writers are excluded. The snapshot object never grants cleanup, unlink, record pruning, retention rollover, reward, replay, session restore or client ACK.

**Hosted acceptance:** build a complete 16-pair/32-record v1 ledger and verify read-only capture leaves every source record unchanged. The *test harness alone* explicitly copies the returned portable text to a temporary fixture file for a distinct OS JVM to independently parse and recapture the same disk ledger, matching trusted SHA-256. Despite successful export the 17th opt-in action remains rejected and no source retention rollover occurs. A `Runtime.halt(127)` writer leaves BEGIN only, and an independent fresh JVM successfully captures its negative incomplete status without deleting/repairing evidence. Tampered manifest fails hash check; a self-rehashed but structurally false manifest fails syntax/count proof. Absent legacy evidence and v2 mixed account evidence cannot be exported; same-length record payload tampering with restored mtime also fails the source validator. No account is modified by export or verification. Focused Java 11 404→405, standalone `g21105MailboxPortableEvidenceArchiveRegression`; stacked PR remains Draft.

**Limit:** This is a portable audit/exchange candidate, **not external durable archival** or safe-retention reclamation. The ledger has a strict finite 32-leaf bound and may deny future opt-in operations. Cross-device hardware power-loss behavior, guaranteed historical writer-population participation, long-term cryptographic authenticity/signatures and deletion/rotation protocol remain unproved. No integration into regular strict/journal/COMMIT hot paths, no ordinary World session adoption, reward claim/grant/replay/release/ACK, no G21.87/88 or frozen R25 #1847 change.
