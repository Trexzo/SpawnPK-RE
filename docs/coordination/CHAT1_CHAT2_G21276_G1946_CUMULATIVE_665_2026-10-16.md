## Gameplay production gap

`LocalCanonicalNpcAttackHandler` previously sampled `attackTick` before an arbitrary pre-resolution callback, then updated player-owned cooldown as `attackTick + attackDelayTicks` after damage. World ticks can advance in between, leaving a just-committed hit with an already-expired cooldown. New source samples `completionTick` after damage and uses the later tick to anchor cooldown. G1945 exercises 12 World ticks during the first hook and the same target; G1946 checks a different canonical NPC using another handler for the same WorldPlayer. Neither changes original client packet semantics, damage/death tick witness, NPC lifecycle ownership nor the legacy CombatState scheduler.
