## Production change (two files; pre-callback fail closed)
1. `LocalLabTerminalStageIsolation.java`: retain a private object-identity `World boundWorld`, with synchronized, one-way `bindTo(World)`. Calling it again on **the identical World** is safe; trying a different World throws `TERMINAL_DIAGNOSTICS_WORLD_MISMATCH`. In `deliver(World, FinalizeResult)`, check identity BEFORE reading a terminal, running a consumer callback or appending a diagnostic entry. An unbound dispatcher also fails closed. This is the actual Java World object, not a name or durable/reused ID.
2. `LocalLabMonsterSpawnerProvisioning.java`: bind any explicitly injected/normal opt-in dispatcher immediately after validating `world != null`, **before** constructing the Monster Spawner service, NPC lifecycle graph, G1 loop, installed terminal observer or activation runtime. No cross-World partially installed graph. Default-off/null case is unchanged; normal property factory creates a fresh unique dispatcher for its World. An injected dispatcher is conservatively sticky to its first World even if graph composition later fails; no unsafe rebind or automatic rollback is offered.
3. No change to `LocalMonsterSpawnerActivationRuntime` read-only snapshots; no new writable external API, TCP, player opcode, G1/ground or G4/G14/G15/G16/G18 behavior, original client, Chat1 G21 account/RAW/OS locks/marker reader, main or R25.

## Strict nonclaims and future work
- This prevents accidental cross-World injection and subsequent wrong-World delivery through this dispatcher; it does NOT prove the event's attacker, owner generation, original SpawnPK kill attribution or atomic name-based consumer update.
- It does NOT give an idempotent delivery receipt. Calling `deliver(correctWorld, priorTerminal)` manually remains an unsupported internal misuse and may replay callbacks; no ledger or durable stage ACK is provided. G1's installed observer/finalization owns normal exactly-one local delivery; DO NOT use this API for recovery.
